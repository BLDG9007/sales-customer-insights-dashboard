SALES & CUSTOMER INSIGHTS - KEY INSIGHTS

1. The West region generated the highest total sales, making it the strongest-performing region.

2. A relatively small group of products contributed a significant portion of overall revenue, indicating concentration among top sellers.

3. Technology products demonstrated strong sales performance and profitability compared to other categories.

4. Sales activity increased over time, although performance varied across different periods.

5. Customer purchasing behaviour differed by segment, with the Consumer segment contributing the largest share of sales.

6. Regional performance differences suggest opportunities for targeted sales and marketing strategies.
